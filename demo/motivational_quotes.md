# Words Worth Remembering

## On Taking the First Step

> "The secret of getting ahead is getting started." — *Mark Twain*

Every ambitious project starts with a single, imperfect action. Stop waiting for the perfect plan and start building.

---

## On Persistence

> "It does not matter how slowly you go, as long as you do not stop." — *Confucius*

Progress feels invisible day-to-day. Zoom out to a month, a year — that is where the compound effect becomes undeniable.

---

## On Creativity

> "Creativity is just connecting things. When you ask creative people how they did something, they feel a little guilty because they didn't really do it, they just saw something." — *Steve Jobs*

The best ideas rarely come from isolation. Read widely, talk to strangers, and let unrelated fields collide in your mind.

---

## On Courage

> "You can't connect the dots looking forward; you can only connect them looking backwards. So you have to trust that the dots will somehow connect in your future." — *Steve Jobs*

Uncertainty is not the enemy — it is the raw material of every meaningful career decision.

---

## On Simplicity

> "Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication." — *Leonardo da Vinci*

If you cannot explain your idea in one sentence, you do not understand it well enough yet. Strip away the noise until only the essential remains.

---

## On Resilience

> "The world breaks everyone, and afterward, many are strong at the broken places." — *Ernest Hemingway*

Setbacks are not detours — they are the curriculum. The lessons you resist are the ones you need the most.

---

## On Building

> "The best time to plant a tree was 20 years ago. The second best time is now." — *Chinese Proverb*

There will never be a perfect moment to start your company, write your book, or learn that skill. Begin today with whatever you have.

---

## On Vision

> "The people who are crazy enough to think they can change the world are the ones who do." — *Rob Siltanen*

Ambition is not arrogance. The willingness to aim beyond what seems reasonable is what separates the remarkable from the forgotten.
