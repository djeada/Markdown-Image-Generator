# NovaTech — Weekly Content Drops

## Tip 1: Automate the Boring Stuff

- Schedule posts **at least 3 days ahead** to stay consistent
- Use analytics to find your **peak engagement window**
- Batch-create content on Mondays — edit and schedule the rest of the week
- Repurpose one long-form post into **5 micro-posts** across platforms

## Tip 2: Hook Them in 3 Seconds

- Lead with a **bold claim** or surprising stat
- Use numbers: *"7 tools that saved us 20 hours/week"*
- Ask a question your audience already has the answer to
- Never bury the value — put the takeaway **first**

## Tip 3: Design for the Scroll

- One idea per slide, **no walls of text**
- Use contrast and whitespace — let the content breathe
- Bold your keywords so skimmers still get the message
- End every carousel with a **clear next step**

> "Content is king, but distribution is queen — and she wears the pants." — *Jonathan Perelman, BuzzFeed*
